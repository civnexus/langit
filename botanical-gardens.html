The Mount Paradise vision

Arboretum & botanical gardens

Building on the variety already growing across Mount Paradise, the proposed arboretum and botanical gardens would create a destination within the destination. The idea is generous and quiet: remarkable specimens, shaded paths, seasonal color and places to stop and look more closely.

The fern walk

Follow
the shade.

Tree ferns create a lush lower canopy around winding paths and volcanic boulders. Small watercourses and sheltered benches could make this one of the most intimate parts of the garden.

Seasonal color

A landscape
that keeps changing.

Tabebuia and jacaranda suggest moments of flowering color within the predominantly green landscape. Carefully composed groves and open lawns would give each season its own expression.

The water gardens

Still water.
Moving light.

Ponds, small cascades and quiet viewing decks would connect the garden to the mountain water story. Natural edges and lush planting keep the experience grounded in the terrain.

The arboretum

Meet the trees.
Know the place.

A living collection could bring attention to the distinctive species already present across the development: cannonball trees, African tulip trees, casuarina, cypress forms and more. Botanical interpretation and thoughtful paths would turn a walk into a small discovery.

A future chapter

Growing from
what is here.

The garden imagery illustrates a proposed direction, rather than completed grounds. The eventual area and routes will develop with the wider plan. African baobab at the entrance and rainbow eucalyptus along the approach remain distinctive anchors in the Mount Paradise landscape.
