Built on what is already growing

Follow the shade.
Notice something new.

The idea is to dedicate an area of the future destination to trees, botanical gardens and quiet walks. Building on the variety already found across Mount Paradise, this could become a place for discovery, rest and a deeper connection with the landscape.

A landscape with character

Remarkable trees.
Thoughtful places.

Arrival

Baobab & rainbow eucalyptus

African baobab marks the entrance, while rainbow eucalyptus accompanies the private approach from the main road.

Seasonal color

Flowering accents

Tabebuia, jacaranda and African tulip trees offer references for selective color within the wider green setting.

Texture & shade

Woodland layers

Tree ferns, casuarina and cypress forms create a varied landscape of canopies, fine foliage and sheltered spaces.

A garden
to grow into.

The long-term concept makes room for specimen trees, shaded trails, resting places and botanical interpretation. Water and the contours of the land would help shape the experience.

This is a future garden chapter, with its area, routes and visitor arrangements still to be developed.
