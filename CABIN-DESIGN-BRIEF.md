# Cabin design direction

Updated 22 November 2026. These are spatial design targets for the next layout study, not measured areas derived from the exterior renderings.

## Shared accommodation brief

Both cabins should function as complete one-bedroom homes for a couple: enclosed bedroom, enclosed bathroom, living room, compact kitchen, indoor dining for two to four, clothing and general storage, and a sheltered outdoor sitting area. Keep the bedroom separate from the living room with full-height walls and a door. Provide bathroom access from an internal circulation space rather than through the kitchen. Resolve ventilation, kitchen extraction, hot water, drainage and service access during the plan study.

## Mountain Cabin

Retain the simple gabled roof, horizontal timber cladding, volcanic-stone base and small front porch. Extend depth and modestly widen the front. Preliminary enclosed-area target: approximately 55–65 m², with the porch additional. Living, kitchenette and indoor table toward the front; private bedroom and bathroom behind. Keep the compact cabin proportions.

## Garden Cabin

Selected direction: Concept A, a simple rectangular cabin with a broad veranda and continuous tiled roof. The private bedroom is on the LEFT within a volcanic-stone enclosure. Living, compact kitchen and indoor dining are on the RIGHT. The approach path arrives from the right directly into the living area, avoiding circulation past the bedroom. The bathroom belongs behind the bedroom, accessed from a shared internal passage.

Working area target remains approximately 75 m² gross enclosed plus a 22 m² veranda. The earlier [spatial study](design/garden-cabin-layout.pdf) is superseded in orientation: it showed the bedroom on the right. Revise that study to match selected Concept A before architectural development. Its dimensions remain exploratory, not approved construction dimensions.

## Next design pass

Start with scaled furniture and circulation studies, then establish external dimensions and wall thicknesses. Test actual bed and wardrobe clearances, kitchen work surfaces, a usable dining table, shower/WC/basin placement, door swings, storage, roof structure and the relationship between glazing and internal partitions. Reconcile revised exterior elevations with the plan before handing the concept to the architect for site-specific design and finalization.

The images establish architectural character and intended room capacity. The Garden Cabin now has a preliminary dimensioned spatial study; neither model is construction documentation.
