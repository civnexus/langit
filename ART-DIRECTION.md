# Artwork direction and generation prompts

Created using the built-in Imagegen tool. Web-optimized JPEG assets are in `assets/`. The two earlier user-approved images were supplied again and incorporated unchanged apart from JPEG encoding.

## Vista lodge — assets/vista.jpg

Cinematic architectural concept rendering for Mount Paradise, luxury mountain community in Trawas East Java Indonesia. The supplied model-villa veranda reference establishes irregular dark volcanic stone walls, warm heavy exposed timber, black framed windows, deep sheltered verandas and lush gardens. Refined larger villa derived from this vocabulary, pitched dark tiled roofs, elegant pool terrace, layered tropical highland horticulture, tree ferns and flowering shrubs. Wide 3:2 composition, villa at right, pool foreground, green volcanic mountain panorama and distant mist, soft sunrise with warm interior lanterns. Photorealistic textures with painterly light, restrained luxury. No snow, alpine chalet, dominant palms, text or watermarks. Proposed architecture.

## Community vision — assets/masterplan.jpg

Landscape architectural watercolor master vision of Mount Paradise in Trawas East Java, bird's eye oblique view of an intimate garden neighborhood of individual volcanic stone and timber villas with pitched charcoal roofs, deep verandas, private pools, curving footpaths, central natural pond and small communal garden pavilion, abundant tropical highland trees, gardens and green volcanic mountains. Watercolor and fine graphite on warm ivory paper, muted forest green, moss, terracotta and soft blue, generous paper margins. Buildings occupy a small part of verdant land. Freely imagined conceptual layout, not cadastral survey. No lettering, numbers, labels, road grids, snow or alpine scenery. Wide 3:2.

## Lodge interior — assets/interior.jpg

Cinematic architectural interior concept for Mount Paradise Trawas East Java. Spacious refined mountain lodge living room with irregular charcoal volcanic stone walls, exposed honey teak roof beams, black steel French doors open to lush tropical highland garden, covered veranda and private plunge pool. Warm linen sofas, handmade timber coffee table with ceramic coffee cups and tea pot, woven rugs, antique brass lanterns, restrained Indonesian craftsmanship. Green volcanic mountain view, soft misty morning daylight, warm tactile materials, luxurious and lived-in, editorial architecture photography. Wide 3:2, no text, watermark or snow. Proposed design.

## Evening gathering — assets/evening.jpg

Cinematic architectural lifestyle concept for Mount Paradise, tropical highlands of Trawas East Java at blue hour. Intimate landscaped natural pond reflecting warm lanterns, refined circular sunken stone fire lounge set back from water, cream outdoor seating, small contained fire, three relaxed adults, one playing acoustic guitar. Warmly lit volcanic stone and exposed teak timber lodge with pitched dark tiled roof and covered veranda, dense tree ferns, flowering shrubs, rich horticulture and green volcanic mountain silhouettes. Warm amber lights against cool teal dusk, editorial photography, wide 3:2. No snow, tents, text or watermark.
